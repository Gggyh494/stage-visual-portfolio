## ImageGen 生成提示

### afterimage

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium independent CGI studio website hero and live-visuals case cover, wide landscape 16:9. Create a cinematic photorealistic CG still of an imaginary electronic music show inside a large black industrial performance hall. A single huge ultra-wide LED screen displays sculptural, flowing iridescent silver and electric-violet ribbons that read as a physical field of light. A low central stage silhouette, a sparse audience at the very bottom for scale, and restrained violet beams in realistic soft haze. Well-composed frontal, slightly low 24mm wide shot with deep black margins, strongest detail near center; keep bottom left dark for website overlay. Contemporary creative direction, contrasted rich black with violet and occasional cold white highlights, tactile metallic finish, premium rather than sci-fi game art. Edge-to-edge photograph-like still with no words, signage, logos, typography, watermark, collage, or borders.

### liquid

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: commercial CGI case cover for a high-end product launch, landscape 4:3. A single unbranded sculptural perfume bottle in rich oxblood transparent glass with a minimal chrome closure, balanced in a slow suspended fold of liquid metal on a smoked dark-burgundy studio backdrop. The object is the unmistakable hero, around 45% frame height, centered and slightly tilted. Moody high-contrast studio product photography, pristine optical caustics and sculpted softbox highlights, subtle grain, grounded premium fragrance campaign art direction. Color restrained to oxblood, black cherry, warm pale silver. Edge-to-edge render, no labels, no words, no logos, no watermarks, no border.

### signal

In the center, large leaning transparent glass fins create repeated optical refraction bands, wrapping around a quiet cyan luminous volume; polished black floor and deep perspective make scale clear. The installation is the focus and remains visible in a square crop. Edge to edge, no words, no logos, no watermark, no frame.

### led

Controlled electric violet light and silver specular highlights, subtle granular haze, strong silhouette and rich deep black, premium stage CGI. Compose all the important visual detail inside the central 30 percent of frame height, continuous flowing forms stretching across the entire long width so it can read on a very short LED wall. Do not add any frame or bars. No text, letters, logo, watermark, grid or collage.

## 扩展样板的生成记录

新增的舞台与主屏图由内置 ImageGen 生成。每个项目包含一张 1440 × 810 的观众视角全景 `stage.webp`，以及一张 1536 像素宽的大屏原画 `led.webp`。原画按下表的比例从生成稿裁取并压成 WebP。图像与动画均为概念样板。

| 目录 | 项目与画幅 | 原画与展映的动态设计 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `media/projects/ember-grid/` | 熔金脉阵 · 3:1 | 琥珀光沿铜质竖片成排传递 |
| `media/projects/ice-arc/` | 冰弧巡游 · 32:9 | 钴蓝雾中叠放冰白弧线，细光横向掠过 |
| `media/projects/porcelain-axis/` | 白瓷轴线 · 4:1 | 香槟色细线扫过象牙白瓷片的叠层 |
| `media/projects/lime-beacon/` | 荧柠信标 · 3:1 | 荧绿点阵聚成环形信标，扫描线纵向往复 |
| `media/projects/coral-tide/` | 珊瑚引力 · 32:9 | 珊瑚色半透膜叠合，细波纹沿画幅舒展 |

每个目录内的 `cue.mp4` 和 `cue-clean.mp4` 均以该项目的 `led.webp` 为底图，沿用 `stage-motion` 的 HyperFrames 合成方法。每段 7 秒，在底图上叠加程序化光迹和镜头位移。标题版加入工作室标识文字、章节号、英文项目名和画幅标注。无字版关闭文字层，保留同一时间轴和画幅。

`media/hero/` 下的循环片由 HyperFrames 合成，统一为 1280 × 720。8 个项目的循环片各 9 秒，同名新项目使用各自的 `stage.webp`。余像现场、液态序曲和暗室回声分别使用原有概念图 `afterimage-wide.webp`、`launch.webp` 和 `signal-wide.webp`。镜头缩放、微移与扫光在一圈内回到同一相位。`refract-loop.mp4` 为 12 秒默认循环，依次叠化余像现场、熔金脉阵和珊瑚引力的舞台全景，再接回余像现场。所有新增视频用 FFmpeg 导出为无音轨 H.264 MP4，每段低于 1.5 MB。
