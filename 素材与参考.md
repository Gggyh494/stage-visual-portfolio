## ImageGen 生成提示

### afterimage

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium independent CGI studio website hero and live-visuals case cover, wide landscape 16:9. Create a cinematic photorealistic CG still of an imaginary electronic music show inside a large black industrial performance hall. A single huge ultra-wide LED screen displays sculptural, flowing iridescent silver and electric-violet ribbons that read as a physical field of light. A low central stage silhouette, a sparse audience at the very bottom for scale, and restrained violet beams in realistic soft haze. Well-composed frontal, slightly low 24mm wide shot with deep black margins, strongest detail near center; keep bottom left dark for website overlay. Contemporary creative direction, contrasted rich black with violet and occasional cold white highlights, tactile metallic finish, premium rather than sci-fi game art. Edge-to-edge photograph-like still with no words, signage, logos, typography, watermark, collage, or borders.

The object is the unmistakable hero, around 45% frame height, centered and slightly tilted. Moody high-contrast studio product photography, pristine optical caustics and sculpted softbox highlights, subtle grain, grounded premium fragrance campaign art direction. Color restrained to oxblood, black cherry, warm pale silver. Edge-to-edge render, no labels, no words, no logos, no watermarks, no border.

### signal
